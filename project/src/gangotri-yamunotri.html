Overview

Gangotri and Yamunotri, the source shrines of the Ganga and Yamuna, are the westernmost of the Char Dham and a natural pair for a shorter Himalayan yatra. Starting and ending in Delhi or Dehradun, Vardaan takes care of every permit, stay, and meal so your family can give itself fully to darshan. We pace the journey gently for elders and keep certified Garhwali guides with you throughout.

Best time to visit

The Gangotri and Yamunotri temples open around late April / May (Akshaya Tritiya) and close near Diwali / early November. May–June and September–October offer the most comfortable weather. The monsoon months of July–August bring rain and occasional landslides, so we travel with extra care and buffer days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the yatra suitable for senior citizens? +

Yes. We pace the journey gently, choose stays close to the temples, and can arrange ponies or palki for the Yamunotri walk. Do tell us about any medical conditions in advance.

How much walking is involved? +

Yamunotri involves a 5–6 km trek each way (or pony/palki) from Janki Chatti. Gangotri temple is a short walk from the parking area.

Can this be combined with Kedarnath and Badrinath? +

Yes — this is often paired with our Do Dham (Kedarnath & Badrinath) package to form the full Char Dham circuit. Ask us about combining the two.
