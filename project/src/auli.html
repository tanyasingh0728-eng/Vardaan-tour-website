Overview

Auli, at about 2,800m above Joshimath, is India's best-known ski destination, with oak and deodar forest opening onto wide slopes that look straight at Nanda Devi (7,816m), Kamet, Trishul, Dronagiri and Hathi-Ghoda Parbat. In winter the slopes are under snow and open for skiing and snowboarding; from spring to autumn they turn into green meadows. The trip includes the walk to the Gorson Bugyal meadow, the chair lift and artificial lake, and a visit to the Narsingh temple in Joshimath. Starting and ending in Delhi or Dehradun, Vardaan takes care of every stay, meal, and local guide. It is an easy trip suited to families, with snow from December to March.

Best time to visit

For snow and skiing, come between late December and March, with January and February usually bringing the deepest cover; ski courses run in this window too. April–June is pleasant and green, good for families and the Gorson Bugyal walk. September–November brings the clearest views of Nanda Devi. The July–August monsoon makes the hill roads prone to landslides. Snowfall can close the road above Joshimath for a few hours at a time, so we keep a buffer in winter plans.

Frequently asked questions

Will there be snow in Auli? +

Snow is most reliable from late December to early March. Exact dates vary each year, so tell us your travel month and we will advise based on current conditions.

Can beginners ski in Auli? +

Yes. Equipment can be rented on the slopes, and local instructors offer short beginner sessions as well as multi-day courses. Ski rental and lessons are paid on the spot and are not part of the package.

Is this trip suitable for senior citizens and children? +

Yes. Most of the trip is by road, and the Gorson Bugyal walk is optional and can be shortened. In winter we suggest warm clothing and an extra rest day for those sensitive to cold.

Do you pick up from Dehradun? +

Yes — we pick up and drop at Delhi or at Dehradun (railway station or Jolly Grant airport). From Dehradun the trip is shorter, as you skip the drive from Delhi.

Can we add Badrinath to this trip? +

Yes, between May and November when the temple is open. Badrinath is about 45 km beyond Joshimath, so it adds one day to the trip.
