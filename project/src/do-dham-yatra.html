Overview

The Do Dham — Kedarnath and Badrinath — is a shorter, focused version of the Char Dham circuit for travellers with limited time. Starting and ending in Delhi, Vardaan takes care of every permit, stay, and meal so your family can give itself fully to darshan. We pace the journey gently for elders, support you at altitude, and keep certified Garhwali guides with you throughout.

Best time to visit

The Char Dham temples open around late April / May (Akshaya Tritiya) and close near Diwali / early November. May–June and September–October offer the most comfortable weather. The monsoon months of July–August bring rain and occasional landslides, so we travel with extra care and buffer days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the yatra suitable for senior citizens? +

Yes. We pace the journey gently, choose stays close to the temples, and can arrange ponies or palki for the Kedarnath climb. Do tell us about any medical conditions in advance.

Can we do Kedarnath by helicopter? +

Yes — helicopter tickets are subject to availability and weather. Let us know and we'll arrange them as an add-on to your package.

How fit do I need to be? +

Kedarnath involves a 16–18 km trek (or pony/heli). The other dhams need only short walks. Basic fitness and acclimatisation are enough for most travellers.
