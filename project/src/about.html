Vardaan Tour is the pilgrimage arm of Vardaan Rent a Car — helping families undertake meaningful yatras across the Himalayan dhams of Uttarakhand.

Our story

We carry the logistics, so you can carry your devotion

Vardaan began on the roads of Uttarakhand, driving pilgrims safely to temples that sit at the edge of the Himalayas. Over the years we learned that a good yatra is about far more than transport — it is permits arranged in time, a warm room after a long climb, a sattvic meal, and someone who answers the phone at 2,000 metres.

Today we plan complete pilgrimages — Char Dham, Kedarnath, Badrinath and beyond — with certified Garhwali guides, trusted stays, and care for the elders who so often lead our groups in prayer.
