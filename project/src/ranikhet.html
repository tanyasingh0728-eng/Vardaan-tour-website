Overview

Ranikhet is a quiet cantonment hill town wrapped in pine and oak forest, with the Chaubatia gardens' apple orchards and clear views of the western Himalayan peaks. Starting and ending in Delhi, Vardaan takes care of every stay, meal, and local guide for an easy, restful short break.

Best time to visit

Ranikhet is open year-round. March–June and September–November offer the clearest mountain views. December–January can bring light snow, and the monsoon months of July–August bring rain and mist.
