Overview

Nainital, the lake town of Kumaon, sits around the emerald Naini Lake at about 2,000m, ringed by forested hills. Just 17 km away, in a quiet bend of the Shipra river, lies Kainchi Dham — the ashram of Neem Karoli Baba, visited by devotees from across the world. This easy trip pairs boating on Naini Lake and darshan at the Naina Devi temple with a peaceful morning at Kainchi Dham and a day among the lakes of Bhimtal, Sattal and Naukuchiatal. Starting and ending in Delhi, Vardaan takes care of every stay, meal, and local guide. It is a gentle, non-trekking trip suited to all ages.

Best time to visit

Nainital is pleasant from March to June and again from September to November, when the lake views are clearest. Winters (December–February) are cold, with occasional snowfall, and the July–August monsoon brings heavy rain to the hill roads. Kainchi Dham is open all year; it is busiest around its foundation day on 15 June, when lakhs of devotees visit, so we plan timings carefully around that date.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Nainital from Delhi? +

Nainital is roughly 300 km from Delhi — about 7 to 8 hours by road via Moradabad and Haldwani, with a comfortable breakfast and lunch stop on the way.

How far is Kainchi Dham from Nainital? +

Kainchi Dham is about 17 km from Nainital on the Almora road — around 45 minutes by car. We go early in the morning, when the ashram is calm and the queues are short.

Is this trip suitable for senior citizens and children? +

Yes — it is an easy, low-walking itinerary. The Snow View ropeway saves a steep climb, and the ashram is a short, level walk from the parking area.
